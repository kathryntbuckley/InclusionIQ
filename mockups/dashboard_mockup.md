# InclusionIQ Dashboard Mockup

This mockup describes the first visual dashboard concept for InclusionIQ. It is intended to guide future interface design and show how survey analysis results could be presented to HR teams, managers, or organizational leaders.

## Dashboard Sections

### 1. Workplace Climate Summary

**Overall sentiment:** Mixed-positive  
**Responses analyzed:** 15  
**Primary insight:** Employees describe strong team support but recurring concerns around leadership communication, advancement transparency, and comfort speaking up.

---

| Category | Average Score |

| Communication | 3.53 / 5 |

| Belonging | 3.67 / 5 |

---

### 3. Top Themes

| Theme | Signal |

| Fairness | 4 keyword matches |

---

### 4. Key Strengths

- Employees describe immediate teams as supportive and respectful.
- Several responses mention managers listening to concerns and recognizing good work.
- Many employees report feeling included within their teams.

---

### 5. Areas for Improvement

- Leadership communication could be more consistent and transparent.
- Advancement and development pathways need clearer explanation.
- Some employees may not feel fully comfortable speaking up in meetings.

---

### 6. Recommended Actions

1. Create a consistent communication process for policy, scheduling, and role expectation updates.
2. Clarify promotion criteria, development pathways, and internal mobility options.
3. Encourage managers to invite input and respond constructively to employee concerns.
4. Recognize and reinforce supportive team behaviors already working well.

---

## Future Visual Design Ideas

- Score cards for average survey results
- Bar chart for top themes
- Sentiment badge or status indicator
- Recommendation cards for HR or leadership actions
- Downloadable report button

## Ethical Note

Dashboard results should be based only on anonymized and voluntary employee feedback. The dashboard should support organization-level reflection and improvement, not individual employee evaluation.
